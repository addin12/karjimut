Household mail reader

A small private tool used by two people who share one household budget. It lets each of them look up their own bank and payment emails, read-only, from their own computer.

What it does

After a person signs in with Google and allows read-only Gmail access, the tool can search and read messages in that person's own mailbox, for example to check what a bank notification says. It cannot send, delete, label or change any email.

Who uses it

Only the two people in the household, for personal use. It is not a public service, has no other users, and is not for sale.

Privacy policy

Dalam bahasa Indonesia

Alat pribadi untuk dua orang yang berbagi satu keuangan rumah tangga. Setelah masuk dengan Google dan mengizinkan akses baca saja ke Gmail, alat ini bisa mencari dan membaca email di kotak masuk orang itu sendiri, misalnya untuk memeriksa isi notifikasi bank. Alat ini tidak bisa mengirim, menghapus, memberi label, atau mengubah email apa pun. Hanya dipakai dua orang tersebut, bukan layanan umum.

Privacy policy

Household mail reader · updated 22 October 2026

What data it accesses

With each person's own consent (Google sign-in), the tool requests one permission only: read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly). It reads the messages of the person who signed in, and only when that person asks for something to be looked up, for example a bank notification. It never sends, deletes, labels or changes email, and it does not read attachments.

Why

Only to provide the feature the person asked for: looking up their own bank and payment emails for their household budget.

Where the data goes

Google sign-in tokens are stored, encrypted, on the signed-in person's own computer. The tool keeps no copy of email content. When a person asks the AI assistant they use to read a message, that message's text is processed by the assistant to answer that person's question. Email content is not sold, not shared with anyone else, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. The tool has no analytics and no other users.

Your choices

Each person can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can ask for the stored sign-in to be deleted. This is a private tool for two people, not a public service.

This tool's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Kebijakan privasi (Bahasa Indonesia)

Dengan persetujuan masing-masing orang (masuk dengan Google), alat ini hanya meminta satu izin: akses baca saja ke Gmail. Alat ini membaca email milik orang yang masuk, hanya saat orang itu meminta sesuatu dicari, misalnya notifikasi bank. Tidak pernah mengirim, menghapus, memberi label, atau mengubah email, dan tidak membaca lampiran.

Token masuk Google disimpan terenkripsi di komputer orang itu sendiri. Alat ini tidak menyimpan isi email. Saat seseorang meminta asisten AI-nya membaca sebuah email, teks email itu diproses asisten untuk menjawab pertanyaan orang tersebut. Isi email tidak dijual, tidak dibagikan, tidak dipakai untuk iklan, dan tidak dipakai untuk melatih model AI.

Akses bisa dicabut kapan saja di myaccount.google.com/permissions. Alat ini pribadi untuk dua orang, bukan layanan umum.
